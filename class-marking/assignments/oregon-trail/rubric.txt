Suggested rubric, 20 points.
Route (3 points): 3 for most of the line within 50 km of the trail, reaching Oregon City. 2 for mostly on the trail but stopping short or straying. 1 for drawn but mostly off the trail. 0 for none.
Places (5 points): 1 for each place marked within 40 km.
What happened there (10 points): 0 to 2 for each place. 2 for a specific reason the place mattered to travelers. 1 for naming it or a vague answer. 0 for blank.
Why go West? (2 points): 2 for a reason with evidence from the Trails West layer. 1 for a reason only.
Students who used the written option (answers in a note instead of on the map) can earn the Places and What happened there points for their written answers, if the teacher chooses.
