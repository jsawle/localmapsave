Suggested rubric, 18 points.
Places (5 points): 1 for each volcano marked within 50 km.
Tectonic setting (5 points): 1 for each setting given as in the volcano layer.
Last known eruption (5 points): 1 for each year given as in the volcano layer. The teacher may also accept 1991 for Pinatubo.
Pattern (3 points): 3 for the pattern (most are on plate boundaries, many around the Pacific) and the exception (Kīlauea). 2 for one of these. 1 for a vague answer. 0 for none.
Students who used the written option (answers in a note instead of on the map) can earn the Places points for naming the volcanoes correctly, if the teacher chooses.
