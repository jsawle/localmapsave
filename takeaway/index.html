        Things to check

          What you can do with it

          • Look through it. Unzip the file and open index.html. It lists everything you saved, with links.
          • Keep working on a map without an account. Open Atlas, choose Open map, and pick a .mmap file from the maps folder.
          • Show it on a CV. Put the unzipped folder on a website, such as GitHub Pages, and link to it. Check first: the copy includes items you kept private and the data in them.

          On a shared computer, sign out here and in ArcGIS Online when you're done.

          About this app, privacy and versions

          Cost to your school

          None. This page only reads your items. It doesn't run exports, analysis or geocoding, and it doesn't create anything in ArcGIS Online, so no credits are used.

          Privacy

          The page talks only to ArcGIS Online, including Esri's file storage, using your sign-in. Your copy is put together in this browser tab and saved straight to your device; it isn't sent anywhere else, and the page loads no code from anywhere else. Your sign-in is kept in this tab only; signing out cancels it.

          Accessibility

          Built to WCAG 2.1 AA (Section 508): every control works by keyboard, progress is read out by screen readers, and the page follows your light or dark setting.
